Insurance coverage

Trip type

  • “Standard Trip” is a basic travel insurance policy designed for sightseeing trips, vacations, and visits to friends abroad. This policy does not cover injuries sustained while participating in extreme sports, such as skiing or rock climbing.
  • “Study Abroad” is a specialized insurance policy designed for students traveling abroad to study. This policy is not valid in the student’s country of residence and has a minimum term of one year. This insurance is recommended for submitting documents to obtain a student visa in Schengen Area countries.
  • “Working Abroad” is a special insurance policy designed for workers traveling abroad for employment. This policy is not valid in the country of residence and has a minimum term of one year. This insurance is recommended when submitting documents to obtain a work visa in Schengen Area countries.
  • “Cruise” is an insurance policy designed specifically for tourists planning a trip on a cruise ship

What’s covered

Coverage option Coverage detail
Medical coverage Schengen medical travel insurance covers emergency medical assistance, treatment due to injury, medication provision, calling a doctor, doctor's consultation (examination), emergency dental care, exacerbation of chronic diseases, acute respiratory and viral infections, outpatient treatment and inpatient treatment.
Medical transport Schengen insurance includes medical transportation services, emergency ambulance call, independent taxi call to the hospital in the event of an insured event, as well as medical air transportation.
Outpatient treatment Coverage of expenses directly related to the diagnosis and treatment of a specific patient's illness during outpatient treatment.
Inpatient treatment Coverage of expenses directly related to the diagnosis and treatment of a specific patient's illness during inpatient treatment.
Viral and infections Schengen insurance coverage of costs for the treatment of COVID-19 and other viral and (or) infectious diseases includes outpatient treatment, inpatient treatment, medical care, and mandatory observation for up to 15 days.
Pregnancy Schengen insurance for pregnant women includes obstetric care up to the 27th week of pregnancy.
Repatriation Expenses are paid for repatriation of the body to the country of birth and (or) burial in place.
Accidents Schengen insurance provides compensation in case of accidental injury. The payment depends on the insured sum selected in the policy.
Liability Schengen insurance for students includes financial compensation for liability to third parties resulting from harm to life, health, or emotional distress, as well as damage to the property of third parties.
Loss of baggage If your luggage is lost and you are denied compensation from the air carrier, insurance compensation is up to 1,000 EUR. Compensation can be issued only to PIR confirmed.
Flight delay If the flight is delayed more than 4 hours, the insurance company pays for the following hours of waiting in the business lounge.
Loss of documents Compensation of expenses to receive new documents to return to the home country in case original documents were lost during the trip.
Legal assistance Payment for lawyer services abroad during an insured event.
Translation assistance Payment for interpreter services abroad during an insured event.
Deductible Schengen medical insurance has a deductible of 0 EUR.
Activation Schengen insurance becomes effective (activated) after crossing the border or 48 hours from the moment of purchase.
